About RQ Roofing

The local roofer Dane County has trusted for 23 years — and counting.

Since day one

23 Years. One Standard: Done Right.

For 23 years, RQ Roofing, LLC has provided reliable, quality roofing to homeowners and businesses across the Madison area. We're not a storm-chasing outfit that blows through town after hail season — we're based in Sun Prairie, and our reputation is built one neighbor at a time.

Our crews are trained professionals, not day labor. Our estimates are honest — if your roof has years left, we'll tell you. And when we're done, your property is cleaner than we found it: magnetic nail sweep, gutter check, and a walkthrough with you before we call it finished.
